+--Historia--+

A deity that appears in the Kojiki and Nihon Shoki. It is said that he was chosen by the sea god Watatsumi and ferried Hoori-no-Mikoto from Watatsumi's palace to the earthly realm in a single day. Hoori presented him with a small knife on a cord around his neck, which is why his name means "sword-bearer".=Una deidad que aparece en el Kojiki y el Nihon Shoki. Se dice que el dios del mar, Watatsumi, lo eligió para llevar a Hoori-no-Mikoto desde su palacio hasta el mundo terrenal en un solo día. Hoori le regaló un pequeño cuchillo con un cordón al cuello, y por eso su nombre significa "portador de la espada".
